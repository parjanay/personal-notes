# Project Ideas

Capture every plausible project idea here first. Ideas may be incomplete, ambitious, or uncertain; the purpose of this file is to prevent them from being lost.

When an idea moves into active exploration, change its status to `EXPLORING` and link it to a folder under [`project-seeds/`](project-seeds/). Keep ideas that are not being explored as `INBOX`.

## Idea Format

```markdown
### Idea name

- **Status:** INBOX
- **Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Problem or opportunity:** One or two sentences.
- **Why it may be useful:** One or two sentences.
- **Seed:** Not started
```

For an active idea, replace the last two values with:

```markdown
- **Status:** EXPLORING
- **Seed:** [Open project seed](project-seeds/project-name/)
```
